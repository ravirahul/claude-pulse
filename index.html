Settings

Theme

Auto follows your system.

Live plan limits

Gets exact % and reset times from Anthropic using your Claude Code login, every 2 minutes, plus how much of your week went to chats. This is an unofficial endpoint and may stop working. When off, Pulse uses the Claude desktop app's last sync.

Limit alerts

Desktop notification when the 5-hour limit passes 80%, and again when it resets.

Phone access

Open Pulse on your phone over your home Wi-Fi. Phones can view everything and start sessions with Remote Control. The first time, your firewall may ask to allow Node.js on private networks: choose Allow.

About

Claude Pulse shows your Claude plan limits with reset times, every Claude Code, Desktop and Cowork session with one-click resume, and stats on tokens, models, projects and tools. It reads Claude's files on this computer and sends nothing anywhere. Free and open source under the MIT license.
